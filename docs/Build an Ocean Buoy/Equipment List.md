Here is the list of hardware that we are using to build our Ocean Buoy. This may change as we build, learn, and improve.
* [Atlas Scientific ENV-SDS Kit](https://atlas-scientific.com/kits/env-sds-kit/). Includes water temperature, pH, dissolved oxygen, oxygen reduction potential (ORP), and conductivity (for salinity).
* Raspberry Pi Zero. We are building the system on a Raspberry Pi 5 and then will transfer the microSD card to a Zero, which will consume less power.
* Adafruit 9 degrees of freedom [orientation sensor] (https://www.adafruit.com/product/4754)
* 3x 5 watt 6 volt solar panels from [Voltaic](https://voltaicsystems.com/5-watt-panel-etfe/)
* [Adafruit BME688](https://www.adafruit.com/product/5046) - Temperature, Humidity, Pressure and Gas Sensor 
* [Adafruit DC power sensor](https://www.adafruit.com/product/6349) $10.95
* Adafruit TSL2591 High Dynamic Range Digital [Light Sensor](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1980)
* Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 - [12MP 120 Degree Wide Angle Lens](https://www.adafruit.com/product/5658)
* Raspberry Pi [AI Camera](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/accessories/ai-camera.html)
* [12V Battery Solar Power Manager Module](https://wiki.dfrobot.com/dfr0580/) $28.50
